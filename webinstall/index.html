Geberit AquaClean proxy

Flash the Bluetooth proxy for the Homey app, straight from your browser.

The Homey Geberit AquaClean app talks to the toilet through a small ESP32 board placed nearby. This page puts the firmware on that board for you. No ESPHome, no Python, no command line.

You need

  1. An ESP32-C3 board.
  2. A USB cable that carries data, not just power.
  3. Chrome or Edge on a computer (Safari and Firefox cannot flash over USB).
  4. Your 2.4 GHz WiFi name and password.

Plug the board in, click Install, pick the serial port, then enter your WiFi when asked.

After flashing, the installer asks for your WiFi over the same USB cable and the board joins your network. Then open the Homey app's settings and enter the board's IP address (find it in your router). Port 6053.

Placement

Put the board within a few metres of the toilet with clear line of sight. Bluetooth range is the most common source of trouble, and a person sitting between the board and the toilet weakens the signal noticeably.

Full guide and the advanced (baked-in) configuration are in the setup documentation. Firmware based on jens62/geberit-aquaclean.
